FROM A BUDGET TO A JOURNEY

One approval.
Everything accounted for.

For student communities, organizers and sponsors managing travel reimbursements.

01

Reserve

The sponsor funds a token vault for a fixed student and reviewer. The cap and deadlines are fixed after funding.

02

Review

The student submits an expense and an original receipt. A human checks eligibility and the evidence.

03

Pay & return

Solana pays the approved amount and returns the entire unused balance to the sponsor in one transaction.

TripGrant reimburses an expense after it is incurred. The MVP does not provide advance financing. Rejection returns the full balance; after the review deadline, anyone can trigger a refund to the fixed sponsor.

01 / EVIDENCE

Private originals.
Verifiable fingerprints.

The receipt stays off chain and is restricted to the grant participants in the full app. Its SHA-256 commitment is on Solana. A match proves the file matches; authenticity still requires human review.

02 / SETTLEMENT

Fixed participants.
One final decision.

The program checks signers, token accounts, deadlines and caps. A terminal grant cannot pay twice. The unused vault closes after the refund.

03 / VERIFICATION

See the result.
Inspect the execution.
